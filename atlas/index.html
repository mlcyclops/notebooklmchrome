Getting started

  1. Load the Folderizer extension. In your browser open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and pick the bundled extension folder (desktop menu: Help → Connect the extension).
  2. Open NotebookLM. Go to notebooklm.google.com and keep the tab open. The status pill turns green when connected.
  3. Organize & create. Pick a folder, then Plan and Generate episodes for a podcast series, or Generate study pack for guides, FAQ, and timelines.

Good to know

  • Atlas talks only to localhost:3000. Your data stays on your machine.
  • Generation drives NotebookLM's UI and is best-effort. Planning works even before the extension connects.
  • Watch mode can regenerate a folder's material when its notebooks change.
