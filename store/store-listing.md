## Detailed description

> Tired of scrolling a flat, endless list of NotebookLM notebooks? Folderizer adds
> the one thing that has always been missing: real, nested folders you can drag your
> notebooks into, right inside the NotebookLM sidebar.
>
> FEATURES
> - Custom folders: group notebooks however you like.
> - Nested tree: folders inside folders, with accordion collapse.
> - Drag and drop: move notebooks between folders in one gesture.
> - Live search: filter folders and notebooks instantly.
> - Colors and icons: make folders your own.
> - Cross-device sync (optional): mirror your folders across your signed-in Chrome
>   devices. Off by default; no account and no server of ours.
> - Private by design: everything is stored locally in your browser.
>
> POWER USERS (OPTIONAL)
> A separate, optional local companion server turns your folderized library into a
> personal knowledge graph and a content studio (Atlas): export your library as
> JSON or GraphML, and generate study packs and podcast-style audio overviews across
> a folder. The folder organizer works fully on its own; the server is opt-in and
> runs only on your machine.
>
> Folderizer is open source. Feedback and issues are welcome.

## Single purpose (for the dashboard "Single purpose" field)
